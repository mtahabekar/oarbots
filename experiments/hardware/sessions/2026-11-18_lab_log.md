## Blue's arm jitters with its wrist sensor connected (10:20-10:45)

Blue's arm works again (driver: "The arm is ready to use", serial ...260001). The user reported that
it jitters under joystick control whenever its Rokubi is connected.

- **Not software.** In the stock system nothing reads Blue's sensor: Blue's stock Rokubi node exits at
  start (no device alias), and joystick motion runs in the arm's firmware.
- **The first motion test reproduced it** (`blue_20261118_102137`, z at 3 cm/s, sensor on Blue's
  laptop): only 2 of 98 joint readings changed while the guard forwarded 5 cm, then the readings caught
  up in 10.5 and 79.9 mrad single steps (a Jaco2 joint moves at most ~12 mrad in 20 ms), and the script
  stopped on "hand speed 15.5 cm/s". The wrist force swung as on a moving arm (sd 5.8 N). No driver
  error, no USB fault: the arm's controller reported stale angles.
- **The user isolated it with the joystick:** jitter only with **both** the arm's USB and the sensor's
  USB on Blue's laptop. Unplugging either cures it. Unplugging the laptop's charger does not, nor does
  moving the arm's USB from the riser hub straight into the laptop.
- **Reading:** the sensor body is bolted to the wrist, so the two USB cables tie the wrist and the arm
  base to the laptop's ground; motor current then also returns outside the arm's own wiring and upsets
  the arm's internal bus. Silver has the same USB layout (both devices on its riser hub, a self-powered
  Realtek RTS5411) and moves cleanly, so the difference is probably Blue's sensor mount. Not measured
  yet (What is left, 1).
