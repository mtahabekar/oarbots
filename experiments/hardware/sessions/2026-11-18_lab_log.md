## Blue's arm jitters with its wrist sensor connected (10:20-10:45)

Blue's arm works again (driver: "The arm is ready to use", serial ...260001). The user reported that
it jitters under joystick control whenever its Rokubi is connected.

- **Not software.** In the stock system nothing reads Blue's sensor: Blue's stock Rokubi node exits at
  start (no device alias), and joystick motion runs in the arm's firmware.
- **The first motion test reproduced it** (`blue_20261118_102137`, z at 3 cm/s, sensor on Blue's
  laptop): only 2 of 98 joint readings changed while the guard forwarded 5 cm, then the readings caught
  up in 10.5 and 79.9 mrad single steps (a Jaco2 joint moves at most ~12 mrad in 20 ms), and the script
  stopped on "hand speed 15.5 cm/s". The wrist force swung as on a moving arm (sd 5.8 N). No driver
  error, no USB fault: the arm's controller reported stale angles.
- **The user isolated it with the joystick:** jitter only with **both** the arm's USB and the sensor's
  USB on Blue's laptop. Unplugging either cures it. Unplugging the laptop's charger does not, nor does
  moving the arm's USB from the riser hub straight into the laptop.
- **Reading:** the sensor body is bolted to the wrist, so the two USB cables tie the wrist and the arm
  base to the laptop's ground; motor current then also returns outside the arm's own wiring and upsets
  the arm's internal bus. Silver has the same USB layout (both devices on its riser hub, a self-powered
  Realtek RTS5411) and moves cleanly, so the difference is probably Blue's sensor mount. Not measured
  yet (What is left, 1).

**Target.** The simulated layout's grasp joints (`experiments/hardware/cell/grasp.yaml`) put the real tools
where the layout wants them, by the real robots' MoveIt models (`scripts/hw/grasp_target.py`): Blue
(-0.426, -0.413, 1.053), Silver (+0.411, -0.423, 1.059), tool 30 deg down and 45 deg inward,
collision-free; from there each wrist camera sees the other grip, both hands and the sheet's centre.

**Why joint space.** From Kinova Home no straight Cartesian path works: with the Home orientation kept,
IK flips at 56-80 % of the way for every grasp position tried; turning the tool on the way, the path
crosses a wrist singularity (joint 5 -> 0.08 rad at 35-40 %, then joints 4 and 6 spin). The joystick
(the lab's way) was the first plan; the user chose to have the arms moved by software instead.

**Fingers.** The arm driver calls Kinova `InitFingers` at every start ("the fingers should open
completely"; hard-coded `is_first_init`). Once the fingers are initialized it does not reopen them: at a
restart at 17:06 Silver's closed fingers (1.40-1.54 rad) stayed closed. The stock GUI closes fingers with
the finger action only while no velocity stream runs; our guard streams at 100 Hz, and a second stream
into the arm's trajectory FIFO could delay the guard's commands. User: Blue's fingers cannot be driven;
Silver's stay closed all the time; the two robot corners carry **universal binder clips (8-9 g each)** that
fix them on the fingers at the same spot every time.

**Code (deployed to both helpers):**
- `bca5999` guard `hold_fingers_percent` (launch arg `hold_fingers`; 100 in `hosts/silver.args`): while the
  deadman is held the guard sends its arm command with the finger target through the driver's
  `cartesian_velocity_with_fingers` input, one stream. `d99f7e9`: only in the fabric_cbf mode, because the
  deadman is shared by both robots (a hold for Blue at 17:05 closed Silver's empty fingers).
- `2818f3f` guard joint input `commands/joint/arm` (deg/s, the driver's units; fabric_cbf mode, deadman,
  at most 15 deg/s per joint, in place of that tick's Cartesian command; a joint and a Cartesian command
  together trip it) and `scripts/hw/grasp_joints.py` (straight joint-space path at 10 deg/s for the
  largest joint, advancing only while the guard forwards; preflight: MoveIt every degree, 15 cm from the
  other arm; holds the arm after arriving until the deadman is released). Fixes: `e97b225` (the driver's
  idle trajectory controller also has a publisher on the joint input), `325812b`, `13c767c` (below).
