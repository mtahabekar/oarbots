## Bending (cantilever, Peirce / ASTM D1388)

Overhang `l` at which the strip's tip reaches the 41.5 degree line; bending length `c = l/2`;
flexural rigidity `G = W c^3` with `W` = 2.113 N/m^2.

| Strip | Readings l (cm), both ends x both faces | Mean l | c | G (N m) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4.3, 3.4, 3.8, 3.4 | 3.73 | 1.86 cm | 1.37e-5 |
| 2 | 4.3, 3.8, 3.0, 4.0 | 3.78 | 1.89 cm | 1.42e-5 |
| Both | sd 0.46 cm | 3.75 +/- 0.16 (s.e.) | 1.88 cm | **1.4e-5** (1.1-1.8e-5 for mean +/- 2 s.e.) |

The strips' directions were not recorded; they agree within the scatter, so no difference between
the two thread directions is visible. For comparison, the carbon range assumed in the demo plan is
1e-4 to 1e-3 N m (not measured): this cloth bends 7-70 times more easily.

**Consequence for the twin.** The bending length (1.9 cm) is shorter than the twin's mesh spacing
(3 cm on 29 x 29) and far below the estimator's (12 cm on 8 x 8). Neither mesh can show this cloth's
bending: its hanging shape is set by its weight and the grips. The twin's bend coefficients are
therefore not identifiable with this sheet; keep them low rather than fit them.
