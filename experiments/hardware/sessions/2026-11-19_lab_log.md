## Elliptic capsule instead of the box (14:00-15:00) — tried, then removed (16:15, user)

- `src/fabric_twin/fabric_twin/elliptic_capsule.py`: cylinder (x/a)^2+(y/b)^2 <= 1, 0 <= z <= h; cap = the part above
  h of the ellipsoid with semi-axes (k a, k b, C) whose section at h is the cylinder's ellipse; cap height t,
  flatness k >= 1 (1: half ellipsoid; larger: flatter dome). Convex. `distances()` has Capsule's contract
  (signed clearance minus particle radius, outward normal, nearest point), so the SensitivityCbfController uses
  it unchanged. Nearest point on an ellipse/ellipsoid: Newton on Eberly's root equation from a start where it is
  non-negative (monotone, stays in its domain); 12 steps exact to 0.1 um on 25,000 points incl. 6:1 axes, 16
  used; 841 particles ~1 ms. Candidates: side wall (rims included), cap if its nearest point is above the rim,
  floor disc. `fit()`: smallest volume over (x, y, yaw, a, b, h, t, k) with every convex-hull point inside
  (SLSQP, 9 starts from the minimum-area ellipse of the footprint), then + pad on a, b and t.
- Twin: `Obstacle.capsule`; the CBF uses it, the clearance report uses it, the twin sheet collides with its
  surface mesh (Newton mesh shape), RViz draws it (box and cylinder envelope hidden).
  `fabric_sim_isaacsim_newton/capsule_cbf/material_grid.py`: the CBF's coarse prediction model now copies mesh
  shapes too (it accepted planes and boxes only; additive, planes and boxes unchanged).
- Also fixed: the twin mapped the obstacle's yaw into its frame as A R A^T (the conjugated rotation), right only
  when the twin's x axis is world x; now the mapped axis A (R e_x). No effect in this cell so far (robots' edge
  along world x, no mirror).
- `obstacle_detect.py` writes the capsule (`obstacle.yaml`: shape elliptic_capsule, fit summary), its mesh/URDF
