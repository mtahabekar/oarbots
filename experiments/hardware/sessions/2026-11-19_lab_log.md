laptop rebuild of `fabric_twin` used plain `colcon`, which rewrote the twin's launcher to `/usr/bin/python3`;

commands: the arms correctly did not move. Fixed: `fabric_run.sh build-laptop` (the documented build);
`fabric_run.sh twin`/`go` now refuse to start when the launcher is not the venv's.

## Gaps in the written procedure (found while following it today)

1. After a laptop reboot, `cell_up.sh --check` reports a FAIL on the missing 192.168.1.200 because it only
   checks. Plain `cell_up.sh`, or the `nmcli` line it prints, fixes it.
2. The procedure does not say when to use `--deploy`. To repeat a run exactly, do not deploy. The helpers
   keep the last run's code, and deploying rebuilds on them.
3. Expected results per step are not written. Today's values (above) are the reference: recentre ends near
   1 deg, perception ~5 Hz analyses and ~14 Hz glove detection per camera, frame ages under 0.1 s.
4. Between `perception` and `twin`, nothing checks the perception output. Today it was read over ssh from
   the `/dev/shm` logs. `status` covers it only once the twin runs.
5. Bridge logs are appended: read only the lines after the latest "process started".
6. Blue's finger reading (0, not driven) is not stated as the expected value.
